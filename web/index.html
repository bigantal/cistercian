Cistercian numerals

Any number from 1 to 9,999, written as one medieval glyph.

1 – 9,999

How it works

Cistercian monks wrote numbers as a vertical stem with one small mark in each corner. The upper right holds the units, the upper left the tens, the lower right the hundreds and the lower left the thousands. A zero digit simply leaves its corner empty.
