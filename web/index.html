Cistercian numeral explorer

Medieval Cistercian monks wrote any number from 1 to 9,999 as a single glyph: a vertical stem with one small mark in each corner. The upper right holds the units, the upper left the tens, the lower right the hundreds and the lower left the thousands. A zero digit leaves its corner empty.
